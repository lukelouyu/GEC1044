枸杞子 Gou Qi Zi
Goji / wolfberry 
(fruit)
(PolyU, n.d.-b)
Sweet; 
neutral
Liver, 
Kidney
Nourishes Liver and 
Kidney; replenishes 
essence; supports vision.
Dizziness, aching lower back 
and knees, and impaired vision 
in Liver–Kidney deficiency 
patterns.
罗汉果 Luo Han Guo
Monk fruit (fruit)
(PolyU, n.d.-j)
Sweet; cool Lung, Large 
Intestine
Clears heat; moistens 
Lung; lubricates the 
intestines.
Dry cough, sore throat and 
hoarseness in Lung-heat 
dryness; constipation from 
intestinal dryness.
