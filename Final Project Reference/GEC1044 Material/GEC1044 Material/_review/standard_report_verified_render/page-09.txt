黄芪 Huang Qi
Astragalus / 
milkvetch (root)
(PolyU, n.d.-l)
Sweet; warm Spleen, 
Lung
Tonifies Qi; strengthens 
exterior defence; 
promotes fluid discharge 
and tissue repair.
Fatigue, poor appetite and 
loose stools in Qi deficiency; 
spontaneous sweating from 
weak exterior defence.
玉竹 Yu Zhu
Fragrant Solomon’s 
seal (rhizome)
(PolyU, n.d.-g)
Sweet; 
slightly cold
Lung, 
Stomach
Nourishes Yin; moistens 
dryness; generates fluids 
and relieves thirst.
Dry cough, dry throat and thirst 
in Lung–Stomach Yin 
deficiency.
淮山／山药 Huai 
Shan / Shan Yao
Chinese yam 
(rhizome)
(PolyU, n.d.-f)
Sweet; 
neutral
Spleen, 
Lung, 
Kidney
Supports Spleen and 
Stomach; generates 
fluids and benefits Lung; 
strengthens Kidney.
Poor appetite and persistent 
diarrhoea in Spleen deficiency; 
chronic cough in Lung 
deficiency; frequent urination 
in Kidney deficiency.

(fruit)

Sweet; warm Spleen, 
Stomach
Tonifies middle Qi;

the spirit.
Poor appetite, tiredness and 
loose stools in Spleen￾deficiency patterns.
