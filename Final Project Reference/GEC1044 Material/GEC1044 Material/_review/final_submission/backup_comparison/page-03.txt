2.3 Hougang and the customers described by staff
Staff described customers as mainly Chinese, middle-aged and older, including people in their forties
and fifties. Hougang planning area had 228,140 residents in June 2024. HDB separately reported
57,557 dwelling units and 165,450 residents in its Hougang flats as of 31 March 2024. These totals
cover different housing populations (Singapore Department of Statistics [DOS], 2024; Housing &
Development Board [HDB], 2024, pp. 11–12).
Hougang Central, the subzone around the outlet, had about 4,620 residents in June 2025. Official
rounded age-group totals show that around 1,070 residents were aged 65 or above, or roughly 23%
(DOS, 2025). The 647 Hougang respondents in Chua et al.’s (2025) study had an average age of 69
and had lived in the area for an average of 22 years. At the same time, the Hougang region has a
rich Teochew history. The National Heritage Board (NHB) describes the community around Tou Mu
Kung and its fishing, farming and rubber-growing activities (National Heritage Board [NHB], n.d.).
The residential setting in Figure 2.3a and heritage image in Figure 2.3b provide context for the
shop’s mixed range of medicinal products and foods. This relatively older local profile may help
explain why staff described mainly middle-aged and older customers, so our observations may not
fully represent outlets in younger neighbourhoods.
