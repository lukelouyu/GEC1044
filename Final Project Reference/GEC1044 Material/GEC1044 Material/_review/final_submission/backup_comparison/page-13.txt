Cactus and roselle illustrate possible local cultivation, although the origin of the shop’s stock remains
unverified. Mind Soothe Capsules provide a documented Singapore manufacturing link. The
branded Five Flower Tea and monk-fruit drink examples are external industry comparisons
illustrating local packing and beverage manufacture; they are not products documented at Tong
Keow. This distinction allows the table to compare different forms of local involvement without
treating ingredient origin, packing and manufacture as interchangeable.
Finished Chinese Proprietary Medicines (CPM), including capsules and tablets, require the relevant
dealer licences and product listing for sale and supply in Singapore (HSA, 2026b). Local
manufacturing and assembly facilities must also meet HSA’s Good Manufacturing Practice
requirements (HSA, 2026c). HSA’s dataset lists Mind Soothe Capsules (舒肝解郁胶囊), under the
Nature’s Green (绿叶) brand, with Tong Jum Chew Pte Ltd as manufacturer and Singapore as the
manufacturing country (HSA, 2025b). This establishes a manufacturing link, not the origin of every
ingredient. Under Singapore’s regulatory classification, raw or simply processed herbs intended for
traditional medicinal use fall within Chinese medicinal materials; when permitted materials are
incorporated into culinary soup packs or herbal drinks for non-medicinal use, the finished items are
classified as food products. Their classification therefore depends on their form and intended use
(Singapore Food Agency, 2025).
