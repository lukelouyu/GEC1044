4.3 Products for daily pressures
Staff linked daily pressures, such as late nights, long work hours and prolonged phone use, to
staff-reported demand for liver-care or mood-support products. Products marketed with the language
of soothing Liver-related constraint (疏肝解郁)—such as the pictured Nature’s Green Mind Soothe
Capsules, listed by HSA as a Singapore-manufactured Chinese Proprietary Medicine (HSA,
2025b)—show how retail TCM responds to everyday concerns about stress and mental well-being.
These product descriptions connect TCM terms with everyday concerns about stress. They should
not be read as a clinical diagnosis or evidence that the products treat mental illness.
A survey provides broader context for this retail language. In a report on ADP’s 2023 survey, Kumari
(2024) states that 68% of Singapore workers experienced work-related stress at least weekly,
compared with 61% across the surveyed Asia-Pacific markets. We see the shop’s product language
as connecting TCM to familiar concerns and routines. Together with Section 4.1, this suggests that
lifestyle pressures, as well as convenience, help explain the staff-described shift towards packaged
products.
