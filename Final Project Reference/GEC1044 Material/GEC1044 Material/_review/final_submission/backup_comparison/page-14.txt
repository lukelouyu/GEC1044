5.2 Packaging and the language of trust
Packaging identifies a product’s contents and source. HSA’s Chinese Proprietary Medicines
guidelines require full English labelling. Chinese may be added; information in other languages must
match the approved English labelling. Labels must provide product and ingredient details, batch and
expiry information, and the relevant manufacturer, importer or assembler details (HSA, 2025a,
section 5). These requirements make the product and its supply chain more traceable. Product
listing addresses safety and quality requirements (HSA, 2026b), while HSA’s consumer guidance
also notes possible interactions with other medicines (HSA, 2026d). For this report, traceable
product information supports an analysis of how trust is communicated; it does not establish
suitability or treatment effectiveness for an individual shopper.
