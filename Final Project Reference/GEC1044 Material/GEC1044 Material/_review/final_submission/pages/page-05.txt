Pre-selected soup packs shift selection and portioning away from buyers, while households still
cook. Capsules reduce preparation further. Staff explained ingredient uses, and a photographed
multi-buy promotion presented packs through a familiar supermarket offer. Buyers can start with a
prepared combination instead of choosing herbs and quantities. Convenience changes the work
needed to bring traditional ingredients into household routines.
4.2 Products for daily pressures
Staff linked late nights, long work hours and prolonged phone use to demand for liver-care or
mood-support products. Nature’s Green Mind Soothe Capsules use the language of soothing
Liver-related constraint (疏肝解郁) and are listed by HSA as a Singapore-manufactured Chinese
Proprietary Medicine (CPM) (Health Sciences Authority [HSA], 2025b). Such descriptions connect
TCM terminology with familiar concerns about stress and mental well-being. These are retail
associations, rather than clinical diagnoses or evidence that the products treat mental illness.
Kumari’s (2024) report on ADP’s 2023 survey found weekly work-related stress among 68% of
Singapore workers, compared with 61% across surveyed Asia-Pacific markets. This gives broader
context to the shop’s product language, although it does not measure Tong Keow customers’
reasons for purchase. Together with Section 4.1, the staff account suggests that lifestyle pressures
and convenience help explain demand for packaged products: familiar concerns are expressed
through traditional terms, while capsules offer a preparation format that fits daily routines.
4.3 Preparation effort and staff-described lower-demand products
Lower-demand examples help explain the appeal of simpler products. Staff named Ban Zhi Lian (半
枝莲), specialist herbs requiring combinations and strong tonic formulas (大补方) (Table A4). They
linked lower appeal to unfamiliarity and preparation effort. Staff also linked stronger tonics’ lower
appeal to a local preference for lighter qing bu (清补) in Singapore’s hot and humid climate. They
reported stocking fewer slow-selling items. These comments concern retail demand, not medical
unsuitability. Unfamiliar combinations may need more advice and preparation. Prepared packs
reduce these demands, helping explain staff-reported differences in appeal.
5 Local adaptation through products, packaging and cultural contact
5.1 Imported materials and different local connections
Staff explained that most imports come from China because of the wider variety and lower cost,
while local cultivation remains limited. Table A5 presents five examples of Singapore-linked products
and organizes them by cultivation, manufacture, processing or packing, and branding. Local
manufacture and local cultivation are separate stages: ingredients may be grown elsewhere while
manufacturing takes place in Singapore. Each stage therefore represents a different kind of
connection with Singapore. These distinctions show that localisation can occur through production
and retail practices even when cultivation remains overseas (Yang, 2022).
