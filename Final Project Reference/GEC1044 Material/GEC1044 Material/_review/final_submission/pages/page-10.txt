淮山／山药 Huai
Shan / Shan Yao
Chinese yam
(rhizome)
Sweet;
neutral
Spleen,
Lung, Kidney
Huai Shan supports
the Spleen and
Stomach. It
generates fluids,
benefits the Lung
and strengthens the
Kidney.
Poor appetite and persistent
diarrhoea in Spleen
deficiency; chronic cough in
Lung deficiency; frequent
urination in Kidney
deficiency.
红枣／大枣 Hong
Zao / Da Zao
Red dates/jujube
(fruit)
Sweet;
warm
Spleen,
Stomach
Red dates tonify
middle Qi, nourish
Blood and calm the
spirit.
Poor appetite, tiredness and
loose stools in
Spleen-deficiency patterns.
枸杞子 Gou Qi Zi
Goji/wolfberry (fruit)
Sweet;
neutral Liver, Kidney
Goji nourishes the
Liver and Kidney. It
replenishes essence
and supports vision.
Dizziness, aching lower
back and knees, and
impaired vision in
Liver–Kidney deficiency
patterns.
罗汉果 Luo Han
Guo
Monk fruit (fruit)
Sweet; cool Lung, Large
Intestine
Monk fruit clears
heat, moistens the
Lung and lubricates
the intestines.
Dry cough, sore throat and
hoarseness in Lung-heat
dryness; constipation from
intestinal dryness.
