半枝莲 Ban Zhi Lian
Barbated skullcup herb
References describe Ban Zhi Lian as clearing
heat/toxins, resolving stasis and promoting urination.
They list sores, throat swelling and oedema. Staff linked
lower demand to unfamiliarity and the need for
combinations.
Specialist herbs
Staff said these herbs needed combinations and
guidance. They named no specific formula. The
reference discusses selecting and decocting combined
herbs.
大补方
Strong tonic formulas
Staff said strong tonics for intensive replenishment had
lower appeal than qing bu. They linked this preference
to climate, but did not establish universal unsuitability.
We recorded no formula. Constitution and condition
guide selection.
