Benefits:
● Reduces excessive internal heat
● Promotes urine production and drains dampness
● Reduces inflammation and improves skin complexion
● Clears liver heat to improve blurry vision, soothe bloodshot eyes, alleviate dizziness, and relieve vexation or feelings of irritability.
● Relieves damp-heat symptoms such as lethargy, feeling of heaviness, loose stools or diarrhea, abdominal distension, burning sensation on urination, restlessness, etc.
● Alleviates wind-heat cough or flu symptoms such as sore throat, feeling of thirst, runny nose with yellow nasal discharge, cough with sticky yellow phlegm, dry and hard stools, yellow urine, etc.
● Promotes healthy blood sugar, blood cholesterol, and blood pressure levels

Suitable for whom:
Recommended for individuals who frequently keep late nights, overwork, or consume excessive cold, raw, spicy, fried, and oily greasy foods. It is also suitable for vegetarians.

Recommended intake:
Take 1-2 servings a day, once a week. Avoid drinking 2 hours before bedtime. Children aged 3-7 should consume half the recommended intake.

Frangipani, Capillary Wormwood Herb, Kapok, Sophora Flower, Chrysanthemum, Honeysuckle

鸡蛋花 jī dàn huā、茵陈 yīn chén、木棉花 mù mián huā、槐花米 huái huā mǐ、菊花 jú huā、金银花 jīn yín huā

Product of Singapore

Preparation:
Rinse herbs before use. Add sufficient water to cover the herbs in a pot. Bring it to a boil and simmer for 15 minutes. Serve it warm.

Serving size:
3 to 4 servings

Storage:
Keep refrigerated.

In Chinese medicine, it is believed that our lifestyle and eating habits can affect how we experience and adapt to seasonal changes. When our body's natural defenses (guarded by the Wei Qi 卫气) are unable to combat external pathogens such as wind, heat, and cold, it can lead to the common cold. TCM classifies cold based on two types: wind-cold flu and wind-heat flu. The former occurs more commonly during cold weather or due to cold environments, while the latter occurs more often during the hot and dry seasons.

Symptoms:
● Wind-cold flu: Aversion to cold, runny nose with clear mucus, body chills, fever, little or no sweating, cough with clear phlegm, etc.
● Wind-heat flu: Runny nose with yellow nasal discharge, cough with sticky yellow phlegm, sore throat, sweating, dry and hard stools, feeling of thirst, yellow urine, etc.

Our Five Flower Tea makes a great cooling drink to alleviate wind-heat flu symptoms.

Note:
The information above serves as a general guide only. Please consult a licensed TCM practitioner for a more accurate diagnosis and treatment.
