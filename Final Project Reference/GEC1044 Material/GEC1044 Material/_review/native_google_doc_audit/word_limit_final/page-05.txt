4.3 Products for daily pressures
Staff linked late nights and stress to mood-support products. Nature’s Green Mind Soothe Capsules
have a documented Singapore manufacturing link (HSA, 2025b); retail language does not establish
diagnosis.
Reported workplace stress provides context (Kumari, 2024). Lifestyle pressures and convenience
together help explain staff-described demand for packaged products.
4.4 Preparation effort and staff-described lower-demand products
Staff associated lower demand with unfamiliarity, preparation and stronger tonification, stocking
slower-selling items sparingly (Table A4). Lighter options were described as more appealing locally.
