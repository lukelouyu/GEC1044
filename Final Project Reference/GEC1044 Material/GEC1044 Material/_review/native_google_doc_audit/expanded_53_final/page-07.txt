Reference profiles and external examples are distinguished from field observations. Sales records,
customer interviews and branch comparisons would strengthen the analysis.
8 Conclusion
Tong Keow keeps traditional ingredients approachable through household foods, preparation
services and recognisable packaging. Soup displays connect herbs with cooking and light
nourishment; portioned packs reduce household effort. Local branding and confirmed manufacture
link products with Singapore’s setting, while mixed foods create access across communities.
These adaptations support everyday visibility and opportunities for use, rather than demonstrating
clinical effectiveness. Retail access and clinical care remain distinct: treatment requires a qualified
practitioner to assess the person’s pattern of signs and symptoms.
