黄芪 Huang Qi
Astragalus/milkvet
ch (root)
Sweet;
warm
Spleen,
Lung
Tonifies Qi;
strengthens exterior
defence; promotes
fluid discharge and
tissue repair.
Fatigue, poor appetite and
loose stools in Qi
deficiency; spontaneous
sweating from weak
exterior defence.

淮山／山药 Huai
Shan / Shan Yao
Chinese yam
(rhizome)
Sweet;
neutral
Spleen,
Lung,
Kidney

and Stomach;
generates fluids and
benefits Lung;
strengthens Kidney.
