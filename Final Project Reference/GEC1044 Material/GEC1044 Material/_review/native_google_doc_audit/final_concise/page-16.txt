Cactus 仙人掌
Reference cactus Opuntia
cochenillifera is native to
Mexico/Central America.
The interview did not
identify the shop’s cactus
species or stock origin.
Raw-plant example: the
local link is cultivation,
rather than manufacture
of a finished medicine.
No finished cactus
preparation was
identified.
NParks describes
propagation from stem
pads and use of the
stems raw or cooked.
This botanical example
does not identify a
commercial producer or
packer.
Roselle 洛神花
Roselle (Hibiscus
sabdariffa) is native to
parts of Africa. The
interview did not identify
the shop’s roselle stock
origin.
Raw-plant example: local
cultivation is documented
by NParks. This does not
establish Singapore
manufacture of a
particular packaged
roselle product.
Fresh or dried calyces
can be steeped to make
beverages. No
commercial roselle
brand or processor was
named in the interview.
Mind Soothe
Capsules
舒肝解郁胶囊
Tong Jum Chew lists 16
ingredients, including
bupleurum root, lily bulb
and jujube. Their source
countries and Singapore
cultivation are not
disclosed on the product
page.
HSA lists Tong Jum
Chew as manufacturer
and Singapore as the
manufacturing country
(HSA, 2025b).
Nature’s Green is the
brand of this finished
Chinese Proprietary
Medicine.
Five Flower
Tea
五花茶
The reference blend
contains frangipani,
capillary wormwood,
kapok, sophora flower,
chrysanthemum and
honeysuckle. Wing Joo
Loong does not disclose
their growing countries.
Wing Joo Loong labels
the reference tea
“Product of Singapore”
and “Packed in
Singapore”. Local
packing is documented;
ingredient cultivation is a
separate question.
Wing Joo Loong
markets the pre-packed
blend as Lung Soothing
Summer Tea, formerly
Five Flower Tea, with
unchanged ingredients.
This is an external
product example.
Monk fruit tea
罗汉果茶
Monk fruit (Siraitia
grosvenorii) is native to
southern China and
northern Thailand.
Documented local
example: Heng Foh
Tong’s HFTea Herbal Tea
Cooling Drink contains
monk fruit and is
produced by a licensed
beverage manufacturer in
Singapore.
Heng Foh Tong markets
this ready-to-drink
beverage under HFTea.
It demonstrates local
beverage production; it
does not identify the
maker or packer of Tong
Keow’s tea.
