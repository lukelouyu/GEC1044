淮山／山药 Huai
Shan / Shan Yao
Chinese yam
(rhizome)
Sweet;
neutral
Spleen,
Lung,
Kidney
Supports Spleen
and Stomach;
generates fluids and
benefits Lung;
strengthens Kidney.
Poor appetite and
persistent diarrhoea in
Spleen deficiency; chronic
cough in Lung deficiency;
frequent urination in
Kidney deficiency.
红枣／大枣 Hong
Zao / Da Zao
Red dates/jujube
(fruit)
Sweet;
warm
Spleen,
Stomach
Tonifies middle Qi;
nourishes Blood;
calms the spirit.
Poor appetite, tiredness
and loose stools in
Spleen-deficiency patterns.
枸杞子 Gou Qi Zi
Goji/wolfberry
(fruit)
Sweet;
neutral
Liver,
Kidney
Nourishes Liver and
Kidney; replenishes
essence; supports
vision.
Dizziness, aching lower
back and knees, and
impaired vision in
Liver–Kidney deficiency
patterns.
罗汉果 Luo Han
Guo
Monk fruit (fruit)
Sweet; cool
Lung, Large
Intestine
Clears heat;
moistens Lung;
lubricates the
intestines.
Dry cough, sore throat and
hoarseness in Lung-heat
dryness; constipation from
intestinal dryness.
