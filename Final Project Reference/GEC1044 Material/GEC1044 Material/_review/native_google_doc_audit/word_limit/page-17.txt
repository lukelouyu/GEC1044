Specialist herbs
Used as ingredients in a combination selected for a
person's TCM pattern, rather than as one uniform
product. Preparation may involve selecting compatible
ingredients and quantities, then following instructions for
decoction (simmering herbs in water). Staff described a
need for combination and guidance. Buyers therefore
face more decisions and preparation than with
preselected soup packs, which helps explain lower
demand. The interview did not identify a specific
combination or clinical case.
大补方
Strong tonic formulas
Intended for more intensive replenishment when an
assessed deficiency calls for it; the ingredients and
preparation depend on the chosen formula. Staff linked
lower appeal to Singapore's hot, humid climate and a
preference for lighter qing bu (清补) options. This is a
retail interpretation, rather than evidence that every
strong tonic is unsuitable locally. Unlike a general food
tonic, a medicinal tonic needs selection according to the
person's constitution and condition, adding decisions
about suitability and preparation.
