Medicated oils
Reference Fei Fah Red Flower Ointment is
an external liniment containing methyl
salicylate, turpentine, white oil and menthol
among its listed ingredients. Fei Fah
describes use for sprains, bruises and
muscular aches. This identifies the
illustrated product’s composition and
intended use; the general category
“medicated oils” includes products with
different ingredients.
