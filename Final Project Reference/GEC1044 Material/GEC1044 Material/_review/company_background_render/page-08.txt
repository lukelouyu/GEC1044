Appendix A Detailed fieldwork evidence
Appendix A retains the detailed survey findings from the group presentation. The tables identify 
staff-reported uses, traditional reference profiles and the sources of the photographs.
A1 Eight herb entries with separate reference profiles
The presentation groups ginseng and American ginseng in one entry. Table A1 separates their 
profiles, giving nine rows for the eight reported entries. The field cabinet also labels American 
ginseng separately.
