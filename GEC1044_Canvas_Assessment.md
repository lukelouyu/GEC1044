## Tutorial Participation: 20%

Raising and responding to questions, active participation in discussion, with special attention paid to the depth and quality of the student’s response.

## Online Forum: 20%

One of the tutorials will be conducted online in the form of a discussion forum. You are required to respond to the discussion topics posted online.

## Field Work (Group): 30%

Singapore's TCM is closely related to the many medicine halls located in different parts of this country. There are the larger TCM hospitals which provide comprehensive healthcare for many. But most of the the day-to-day TCM experience of the people is really confined to the medicine halls, traditionally known as yao cai pu 药材铺. Many of you would have visited these medicine halls before or maybe not. This is perhaps a good opportunity to find out more about these places. (*Hint:* And since your final project requires the use of TCM recipes, why not seek the help of the professionals in the medicine hall?)

### Group Presentation

For this assignment, you will formed into groups (not more than 5 persons) and visit a medicine hall to conduct a survey. A set of survey questions will be provided. You are of course encouraged to also ask additional questions in your field work. You will then present your findings during the group presentations in the **3rd tutorial**. Please email your presentation materials (Microsoft PowerPoint, etc.) after your presentation to your TA.

### Field Work Report

A field work report is to be completed by your group. The report should be based on the feedback from your presentation. The deadline for submission is week 9 (16 Oct, Friday, 1700hr).

Field Work Report (words limit: 2000 words, including citations, excluding bibliography).

## Final Project (Individual): 30%

Final Project (word limit: not exceeding 3500 words, including citations, excluding bibliography).

### Improving the physical and mental health of university students.

Design a recipe using Chinese medical herbs that addresses common issues such as insomnia, fatigue, stress, loss of appetite, etc. that are experienced by many university students (we know because we asked them). Maybe it is something that you are going through. Or perhaps, it's your friends. Using knowledge gained from the course, you can take control of your health by coming up with a recipe that's supported by Chinese medical theories, and maybe help your friends along the way.

Your project should feature a recipe that you can prepare at home. You are expected to analyse the health issue(s) you have identified using what you have learnt in the course before designing your recipe. A detailed explanation of the medical qualities and health benefits of the ingredients (herbal or non-herbal) is required but **a stronger paper requires good analysis**.

Make sure to photograph the process of preparing the food. You may even shoot a video if you wish.

You may make use of established recipes or use them as a foundation to create your own. Be mindful of the dosage (we are dealing with medicine after all) and how you combine various ingredients. Avoid herbs that are moderately toxic. If you are unsure, do not consume your own recipe, or force your tutors to consume them. Stick to conventional herbal ingredients if possible.

But seriously, have fun doing this!
