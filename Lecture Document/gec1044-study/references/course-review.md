## Self-test with brief answer guide

1. **Why combine disease and syndrome?** Disease supplies course/causes; syndrome captures the present pattern, allowing the plan to reflect the individual and the stage of illness.
2. **Why combine all four diagnostic methods?** Findings can be ambiguous, variable or observer-dependent; cross-checking provides a fuller basis for interpretation.
3. **Can heat and deficiency coexist?** Yes. The lecture's yin-deficiency example includes heat manifestations despite underlying deficiency.
4. **What are the eight principles?** Yin/yang, exterior/interior, cold/heat, deficiency/excess.
5. **How many channels are in Lecture 8's main teaching scheme?** Fourteen: twelve principal channels plus Ren and Du.
6. **How do back-shu and front-mu points differ?** Posterior versus anterior organ-associated point groups in the lecture's framework.
7. **What distinguishes wet from dry cupping?** Deliberate skin puncture/blood removal in wet cupping.
8. **What distinguishes referral from joint case management?** Referral transfers or requests care; joint case management coordinates decisions for the same patient across disciplines.
9. **Does ICD inclusion validate a treatment?** No. Classification and effectiveness are separate questions.
10. **Does an uncontrolled improvement establish treatment causation?** No. Natural recovery, concurrent care and other biases must be considered.

11. **What does Lecture 12 add to the clinical lectures?** A framework for examining state authority, modernity, translation, pluralism and trust.
12. **Does the 76% CAM figure measure current national prevalence?** No. It is a 12-month estimate reported in a 2005 housing-estate survey.
13. **Is standardization equivalent to proven effectiveness?** No. Identity, consistency, safety and clinical outcomes require different evidence.
14. **What is the difference between a historical herb grade and a formula role?** One categorizes a substance in a historical scheme; the other describes its function within a prescription.

15. **Why can Week 1 and Week 4 readings differ?** They make different interpretive arguments; a useful teaching contrast can become misleading when universalized.
16. **How does a text become canonical?** Transmission, editing, patronage, publication, teaching and citation help establish authority.
17. **What unites the four Jin–Yuan masters?** Reinterpretation of inherited theory for particular patterns, despite different clinical emphases.
18. **What distinguishes Singapore's modernization processes?** Community/professional organization from below and statutory regulation from above interacted.
19. **Does a modern institution make a tradition historically new in every respect?** No. Older concepts can be reorganized into new curricula and institutions.
20. **What did the full Studocu comparison add?** Visual details and revision links, with erroneous or overbroad claims corrected rather than copied.
