and other areas of the body brought about by
rheumatism, the book illustrates another
exercise that can be used to treat and alleviate
the pain.

hands together and turn it over in the opposite
direction, lock the knees using your hands,
bending both knees in an angle of five to seven
degrees, grind your teeth, breath, and then
