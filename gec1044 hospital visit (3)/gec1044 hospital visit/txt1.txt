## 先梳理幻灯片关键信息（和情绪/心理健康相关重点）
### 1. 脉诊 Pulse Taking
1. 寸口三部分：**寸、关、尺 (cun, guan, chi)**；左手关对应**肝（肝胆）**，肝主情志，压力情绪问题主要观察左手关脉。
2. 脉的三层取法：浮、中、沉（Superficial / Middle / Deep），医师轻重按压获取脉象。
3. 与情绪、压力相关脉象：
    - **弦脉 wiry pulse**：Stress / anger → more wiry。长期压力、焦虑、易怒、肝气郁结，最典型脉象。【**你的报告核心证据**】
    - 数脉 Shuo pulse(Rapid，>90次/分)：热证Heat Syndrome，熬夜焦虑、阴虚内热，心烦失眠。
    - 细脉 Xi Mai(Thin)：气血不足、阴虚；长期压力耗伤阴血，情绪低落、疲惫。
    - 实脉 Shi Mai(Excess)：实证，情绪激动、肝火旺盛。
4. 很多因素干扰脉象：运动、饮食、体型、年龄、**情绪（压力、愤怒）**。脉象不是静态指标，人的心理状态会即时改变脉相。

### 2. 舌诊 Tongue Diagnosis（望诊）
幻灯片里的舌象，全部对应新加坡年轻人压力相关体质：
- **Qi Stagnation 气滞**：舌尖红，对应焦虑、思虑过度、肝气郁结（压力）
- **Damp Heat 湿热**：红舌，黄腻苔，新加坡最常见；压力+湿热共存，烦躁、口苦、失眠
- **Yin Deficiency 阴虚**：舌红有裂纹；熬夜、长期压力耗伤阴液，心烦、潮热、情绪不稳
- **Qi Deficiency 气虚**：舌胖大有齿痕；长期精神内耗、疲惫，思虑过多
- **Blood Stasis 血瘀**：紫舌瘀斑，长期压抑情绪

>逻辑：中医师**结合舌诊+脉诊，再问诊**，综合判断体质，这就是**辨证论治**。压力情绪不会单独看“心理症状”，而是转化为舌、脉这些身体体征来解读。

# 第一部分：Pulse Taking（脉诊）
## How to take pulse（诊脉方法）
- Cunkou is divided into three portions, namely, cun, guan and chi.
- There is a prominent bone (the styloid process of the radius), which marks the guan.
- Each of these portions corresponds to one of the zang-fu organs and reveals the pathological changes of the corresponding organs.

| | Left hand | Right hand |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| Cun 寸 | Heart 心 | Lung 肺 |
| Guan 关 | Liver 肝胆 (gallbladder) | Spleen 脾胃 (stomach) |
| Chi 尺 | Kidney 肾 | Kidney 肾/命门 (life gate) |
> 3 Positions：Place 3 fingers over radial artery, with middle (guan) position over the styloid process of the wrist.

## Depth of Pulse（脉位深浅）
> 3 Levels: Press fingers over radial artery first lightly, increasing pressure gradually to moderate force, then lastly to deep.
1. **浮脉（Fu Mai, Superficial）**
    - It responds to the finger when pressed gently but feels weak when a heavy pressure is applied. 如水漂木 “as if a log is floating on water”
    - Indications: External syndrome (flu, cold, fever)
2. **沉脉（Chen Mai, Deep）**
    - It only responds to the finger when pressed heavily. 如石沉水 “Like a rock deep in water”
    - Indication: Interior syndrome
      - Deep and forceful: interior excess syndrome
      - deep and weak: interior deficiency syndrome

## Pulse Types（脉象）
1. **数脉(Shuo pulse, Rapid)**
    - More than 5 beats per breath, above 90 beats/min
    - Indications: Heat Syndromes.
2. **缓脉（Huan pulse, Moderate）**
    - relaxed and forceful. About 60-70 beats/min.
    - Indications: Healthy pulse or syndromes of damp, insufficient Qi and blood to move in the vessels.
3. **实脉（Shi Mai , Excess）**
    - Strong beating at all three portions
    - Indications: Excess syndrome, or in healthy people
4. **细脉（Xi Mai, Thready/Thin）**
    - As fine as a thread, but obviously palpable
    - Indications: Qi and Blood deficiency syndromes, Yin deficient

## Pulse can be influenced by（影响脉象的因素）
- Gender: Men (usually stronger and bigger) vs women (usually weaker and thinner)
- Exercise before pulse taking: faster pulse
- Stress/anger: more wiry
- Post heavy meal: more slippery
- Athletes often have a slow pulse, young children have quick pulses.
- Heavier individuals have deeper pulses, while thinner individuals have “stronger”/more superficial pulses.

# 第二部分：Tongue Diagnosis（舌诊）
## Which Tongue Do You Have?
- **Normal**
- **Blood Deficiency**: Pale Tongue, Little or No Coating
- **Heat**: Red Tongue, Thin Yellow Coating
- **Damp Retention**: Swollen Tongue, White Greasy Coating
- **Qi Stagnation**: Red Tip
- **Blood Stasis**: Purple Tongue, Black Spots
- **Damp Heat**: Red Tongue, Greasy Yellow Coating
- **Yin Deficient**: Red Tongue, Cracks, Little or No Coating
- **Qi Deficiency**: Pale Tongue with Red Spots, Thin White Coating
- **Yang Deficiency**: Pale Swollen Tongue, Thin White Coating, Teeth Marks

## Common Tongue Types
1. **NORMAL**：Normal tongue
2. **QI DEFICIENCY**
    - Features: pale tongue with a few red spots, thin white coating, teeth marks
    - Symptoms: Fatigue, Poor appetite, Spontaneous sweating, Shortness of breath, Over-thinking and worrying…
3. **HEAT**
    - Features: red tongue, thin yellow coating
    - Symptoms: Feel hot, Sweat easily, Thirsty, Constipated, Irritable and bad tempered, Skin problems…
4. **DAMP RETENTION**
    - Features: swollen tongue, white greasy coating
    - Symptoms: Bloated, Fullness in chest and abdomen, Feel heavy and lethargic…
5. **BLOOD STASIS**
    - Features: purple tongue, black spots
    - Symptoms: Cold limbs, Varicose veins, Painful legs, Headaches, Chest pain, Liver spots, Lack of skin lustre…
